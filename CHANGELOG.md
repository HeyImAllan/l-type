# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

## [0.1.1] - 2026-02-22

### Added
- Heart pickup healing mechanic: when a non-boss enemy is defeated there is a 1-in-20 chance a heart (sprite 20) spawns at that position.
- Hearts move left slowly, accelerating over time, until collected by Leo or despawned off-screen.
- Collecting a heart restores 1 HP, capped at maximum health.

## [0.1.0] - 2025-10-31

### Added
- Core shmup gameplay with Leo the orange lion as the player character.
- 5 enemy waves featuring bats, orbs, and pumpkins.
- Auditron boss battle with card, fraud, risk, and hack projectile attacks.
- 4-heart health system with invincibility frames after taking damage.
- Afterburner boost ability.
- Scrolling starfield, cloud, and graveyard background.
- Score tracking.
- Game over and win screens.
- Halloween-themed music and sound effects.
